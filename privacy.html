Privacy

Last updated: 11 July 2026

What stays on your device

Your saved quotes and chosen atmosphere are stored locally in your browser. We do not receive this information and you can remove it by clearing this site’s browser data.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand general site usage such as page views, device type and broad interaction patterns. We do not ask for your name, email address, health information or precise location.

Sharing

Sharing happens through the service you choose. Its privacy terms apply after you open that service. Generated quote images are created on your device.

Cookies and control

Your browser may store local preferences and analytics cookies. You can block or clear them using browser settings. Core quote features continue to work without an account.

Contact

No contact address is currently published. This policy should be updated before collecting email addresses or operating a contact form.
